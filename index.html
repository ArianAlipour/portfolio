About

I'm Arian Alipour, a painter based in Tehran, Iran, and a graduate of Painting from the University of Science and Culture. My main practice is painting, but over the years I've explored different areas of visual art. I'm interested in exploring ideas through visual language and experimenting with different ways of making and seeing. Rather than limiting myself to one medium or a fixed way of working, I prefer to let each project find its own form.

This portfolio brings together a selection of my paintings from different stages of my artistic journey. Rather than presenting them as isolated works, I see them as parts of a larger process — a collection of ideas, observations, experiments, and experiences that have gradually shaped the way I paint.

My paintings grow out of my lived experiences and the way I perceive and remember the world around me. I use painting as a way to process these experiences, turning personal observations, memories, and emotions into images. Through this process, I'm interested in moving beyond the purely personal and searching for a shared, experiential narrative — something that can connect my own experience with the experiences of others.

It's a collection of where I've been, what I've explored, and where I'm going next.
